Student Support

BYU Pathway students have access to different resources and support services that can help them succeed in their courses and educational journey.

Ways to Get Support

Academic Support

Use available academic resources, course materials, and instructor guidance when you need help with your studies.

Communicate with Instructors

Ask questions and communicate with your instructors when you need clarification about assignments, lessons, or course requirements.

Stay Connected

Stay connected with classmates and other students. Sharing experiences and encouraging one another can make your educational journey easier.

Ask for Help

Do not hesitate to ask for help when you need it. Using available support resources can help you continue progressing toward your goals.
