Study Resources

Developing effective study habits can help BYU Pathway students organize their time, understand course materials, and make consistent progress toward their educational goals.

Helpful Study Strategies

Create a Study Schedule

Set aside specific times during the week for reading, assignments, projects, and review.

Stay Organized

Keep track of deadlines and important course information so you can complete your assignments on time.

Take Breaks

Short breaks can help you maintain concentration and return to your studies with more energy.

Ask for Help

When you have questions, use available student support resources and communicate with your instructors.
