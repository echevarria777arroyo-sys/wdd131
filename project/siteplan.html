Website Planning Document

Melissa Echevarria Arroyo

Site Name

BYU Pathway Student Guide

This name was selected because the website is designed to help current and future BYU Pathway students find useful academic information, study resources, and student support.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide helpful information for BYU Pathway students, including study tips, course information, academic resources, and contact information.

Scenarios

  • How do I prepare for my BYU Pathway courses?
  • Where can I find study resources and academic support?

Color Schema

  • Dark Blue (#003366) – Header, headings, and footer.
  • Light Gray (#F4F4F4) – Page background.
  • White (#FFFFFF) – Section backgrounds.

Typography

  • Roboto – Headings.
  • Arial – Paragraphs and lists.
